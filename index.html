Click anywhere on the phone. Screen: 1 – Onboarding

Screens

Try these interactions

  • Onboarding: switch language, toggle permissions, then Continue.
  • Home: tap a trail; trails without a pack ask you to download first (Wi-Fi recommended).
  • Trail Mode: press Walk to simulate GPS movement – crossing a stop's geofence auto-plays its narration. Try the transcript, language switch and stop list.
  • Identify: the on-device model builds confidence live; press the shutter when it is ready.
  • Result: switch tabs, play narration, save to Journal.
  • Settings: download/pause packs, change privacy defaults, delete all local data.

Design notes

Offline-first: everything on a trail works with zero signal. Geofencing replaces GPS polling to save battery. Camera frames are processed on the device and never uploaded. Location is requested “only while using the app”.

Rafflesia keithii

Bunga Pakma · world's largest flower genus
Endemic to SabahProtected species
Rafflesia keithii is a parasitic flower found only in Sabah. Blooms last around five to seven days and can reach 90 cm across. It has no leaves, stems or roots and takes its nutrients from the Tetrastigma vine.
